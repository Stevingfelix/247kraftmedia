Thinking Of Getting A Website For Your Business?
Read This Before You Kiss Your Money Goodbye.

Spoilers.

You must be wondering if I'm about to reveal some BIG SECRET.

After all, who would want to blow their hard-earned money on a beautiful website that does absolutely nothing for their business?

Not you, I suppose.

And that's exactly why I'd like you to spare me about 3 minutes of your time.

Close any other browser tabs, turn off notifications, and get rid of anything that could distract you, if you can.

Your full attention is all I need, that’s all…

Because before you hand over your money to a web designer, there are a few things I think you should know.

And no, it's not some complicated technical stuff about websites.

It's something much simpler.

I’m talking about things that could help you make a better decision, avoid some costly mistakes, and understand what you should actually be looking for when hiring someone to build your website.

I will tell you abourrit in a bit

But First, a question….

When you pay someone to build you a professional looking website for your business, what exactly are you paying for?

Are you paying for just a beautiful design?

A collection of pages with your logo and pictures on them?

Something impressive enough to show your friends?

Or Let me guess…

You want a website that not only looks professional but also adds real value to your business

Whether that value means increasing your visibility, attracting more customers, generating sales or even elevating your brand’s prestige.

But ofcourse, your website should look good!

Nobody wants to send potential customers to a website that looks like it was built by 7 year olds as a summer school project.

Sheeesh. I get that…

Infact, a professional-looking website can make a great first impression, help people take your business seriously and give potential customers more confidence in what you're offering.

But here's the thing…

A website that looks good and is actually useful to your business aren't the same thing.

This is where a lot of business owners are being misled.

Most web designers advertise building a sesky looking website that looks professional for your business.

But guess what?

There’s a graveyard full of dead websites that once upon a time, they looked professional but were never renewed after the first year because…

A beautiful, professional looking website can still become a bloooody expense.

And here's where I believe some web designers miss the point.

They know how to make a website look good. The colours, animations, beautiful layouts, fancy effects... all that stuff.

But beyond that, they don't always stop to ask what the business actually needs that website to do.

Things like…

What are you trying to achieve with the website?

Who are you trying to reach?

And how should the website help potential customers understand what you offer and decide whether they want to do business with you?

These questions matter, too.

Because while some web designers have the technical skills to build you a website, they lack the business sense to ask these questions before they start building.

They'll happily discuss your preferred colours, the kind of layout you want, the animations you'd like to see, and how beautiful you want the finished website to look.

And there's nothing wrong with wanting to build a beautiful website.

But knowing how to design a beautiful website and understanding what makes a website useful to a business are two different things.

Now, does asking the right questions mean your website will magically bring in more customers?

Of course not!

A website isn't a magic wand from Harry Potter. 😂

But the point is, if you're going to invest in a website, you should have a clear idea of what you want it to help you achieve.

Because the goal isn't just to own a website.

It's to have something that presents your business professionally, helps potential customers understand what you offer, and makes it easier for them to take the next step.

And that's the kind of website we've been building for our clients.

"But Do I Really Need a Website?"

Honestly? Not necessarily.

Not every business needs a website immediately.

I mean, If your current setup is working perfectly for your business, you might not need to change anything today.

We're not here to convince every business owner on the internet to pay for a website they don't need…

Especially when it’s something you might not have considered necessary until now.

But consider a few things right now my dear business owner…

You’re currently trying hard to build your business.

You've poured your energy, time, sweat (and maybe all your life) into it.

Money, too.

You've got products to sell, services to offer, customers to serve, and a reputation you're working hard to build.

Maybe you use Instagram to showcase your work.

Facebook to reach potential customers.

WhatsApp to answer questions, send prices, and even close deals.

But there's something else worth considering…

How much of your business's online presence depends on platforms you don't control?

Think about it.

Instagram could suspend your account tomorrow.

Facebook could restrict your page.

WhatsApp could ban your number, leaving you scrambling to figure out what happened.

And just like that, you could lose access to the audience you've spent years building, conversations with potential customers, and opportunities to make sales.

Oh dear God.

Imagine spending years building your Instagram page to 200,000 followers, only to wake up one morning and discover you've been banned.

All that time, effort, and money you've invested in building that audience…

And suddenly, you can't even access it.

That's enough to give a business owner sleepless nights.

But here's where having your own website can make a difference in your business…

Your website gives your business another place to establish its online presence, one where you're not entirely dependent on a social media platform deciding whether you can reach your audience.

You have considerably more control over how your business is presented, how your information is organized, and how visitors interact with what you offer.

And while your website still requires maintenance, hosting, and marketing, iit gives you an additional asset you control rather than relying entirely on platforms you don't.

Of course, I'm not saying you should abandon Instagram, Facebook, or WhatsApp.

Far from it.

Keep using the channels that help you attract customers and make sales.

Just don't overlook the value of having a website working alongside them.

Because the goal isn't to have a website simply for the sake of having one.

It's to give your business another useful asset that can help you attract customers, communicate your value, and make it easier for people to do business with you.

So no, we're not saying every business needs a website immediately.

We're simply asking you to consider whether one could make your business easier to run, create more opportunities, and give you greater control over your online presence.

And if the answer is yes, perhaps it's worth exploring what the right website could do for your business.
